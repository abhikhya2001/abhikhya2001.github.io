Hello, I am Abhikhya. I'm currently pursuing my Master of Science in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, where I'm grateful to be advised by Prof. Katerina Fragkiadaki. Previously, I was a Research Associate at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/). I completed my undergraduate studies at the [Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur (IIT KGP)](http://www.iitkgp.ac.in) with a Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Electrical Communication Engineering, and a minor in Computer Science and Engineering. My research interests are broadly in Computer Vision, specifically 3D Vision, Robotics, Reinforcement Learning and Multimodal Machine Learning.
